How It Works

Three steps to your tribe

01

Start with a Workshop

Every WOW Tribe journey begins with one of our workshops. Experience the culture, meet your first tribe members, and discover which track fits where you are right now.

Enquire About Workshops →

02

Join Your Peer Circle

After your first workshop, you're matched into a curated circle of 6–8 women at a similar stage. Your circle meets regularly — online and in-person — to share, challenge, and hold each other accountable.

03

Grow Together, Indefinitely

The WOW Tribe is a long-term community. Members continue through workshops, events, masterminds, and collaborations. The relationships built here follow you far beyond any single session.

Peer Circles

Find your circle

Circles are curated by stage and industry — so every conversation is immediately relevant. Each has 6–8 members and a structured monthly rhythm.

Founders Circle

For women in their first 1–2 years. Validation, early customers, first hires, and learning to trust yourself.

Growth Circle

For businesses with proven traction, ready to systematise and scale. Marketing, team, and operations challenges.

CEO Circle

For established leaders managing teams, entering new markets, or stepping into industry leadership.

Creative Circle

For women in fashion, design, food, wellness, and the arts — the unique pressures of creative businesses.
